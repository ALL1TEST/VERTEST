    60→## 1. Design Philosophy
    61→
    62→### Core Tenets
    63→
    64→**Content-First Design**
    65→The primary purpose of any blog is content consumption. Every design decision must prioritize readability, scannability, and engagement with written and visual content. Decorative elements exist only to support content hierarchy.
    66→
    67→**Progressive Enhancement**
    68→Build a solid, functional foundation that works everywhere. Layer enhancements (animations, complex layouts, interactive features) on top without breaking core functionality for users with older devices or slower connections.
    69→
    70→**Intentional Restraint**
    71→Great blog design knows what to omit as much as what to include. Each element must justify its existence by serving user needs or business goals. When in doubt, remove it.
    72→
    73→**Niche Adaptability**
    74→This system works across all niches because it focuses on universal principles rather than specific aesthetics. The same structural rules apply whether designing for a gardening blog or a fintech publication—only surface-level styling changes.
    75→
    76→**Trust Through Consistency**
    77→Users trust blogs that feel cohesive and professional. Maintain consistent patterns across all pages, interactions, and states. Inconsistency signals amateurism and erodes credibility.
    78→
    79→### Design Hierarchy Pyramid
    80→
    81→```
    82→                    ┌─────────────┐
    83→                    │   Delight    │  (Animations, micro-interactions)
    84→                   ┌┴─────────────┴┐
    85→                   │  Personality  │  (Brand colors, unique components)
    86→                  ┌┴───────────────┴┐
    87→                  │    Function     │  (Navigation, search, sharing)
    88→                 ┌┴─────────────────┴┐
    89→                 │   Readability     │  (Typography, spacing, contrast)
    90→                ┌┴───────────────────┴┐
    91→                │      Content        │  (Articles, images, media)
    92→                └─────────────────────┘
    93→```
    94→
    95→Each layer depends on the layers below. Never sacrifice lower layers for upper layers.
    96→
    97→### Mental Models
    98→
    99→**The Scanning Reader**
   100→Most blog visitors scan before they read. Design for scanners first—clear headings, visual breaks, pull quotes, and highlighted key information. Deep readers will find their way naturally.
   101→
   102→**The Task-Oriented Visitor**
   103→Many users arrive with specific intent (finding a recipe, solving a problem, learning a skill). Reduce friction between arrival and goal completion.
   104→
   105→**The Serendipitous Browser**
   106→Some users explore without specific goals. Create discovery pathways through related content, category browsing, and visually appealing recommendations.
   107→
   108→**The Returning Reader**
   109→Loyal readers develop mental models of your layout. Honor their learned expectations while gently introducing improvements.

   113→## 2. UX Principles
   114→
   115→### Reading Experience
   116→
   117→**Optimal Line Length**
   118→- Body text: 60-75 characters per line (including spaces)
   119→- Maximum width: ~680px for comfortable reading
   120→- Minimum width: ~320px to prevent excessive line wrapping
   121→- Use CSS `ch` units or `max-width` with rem values
   122→
   123→**Vertical Rhythm**
   124→Maintain consistent spacing multiples throughout:
   125→- Base unit: 0.25rem (4px) or 0.5rem (8px)
   126→- All margins, padding, and gaps should be multiples of the base
   127→- Creates visual harmony and predictable spacing
   128→
   129→**Content Density Balance**
   130→- Too sparse: Users scroll excessively, lose context
   131→- Too dense: Overwhelming, feels like wall of text
   132→- Target: 50-60% white space on article pages
   133→- Adjust based on content type (reference material can be denser)
   134→
   135→**Paragraph Spacing**
   136→- Between paragraphs: 1.5em (or 1.5 × line-height)
   137→- Never use indentation for web paragraphs (print convention)
   138→- First paragraph after heading can be slightly larger for emphasis
   139→
   140→### Navigation Flow
   141→
   142→**The Three-Click Rule (Modified)**
   143→Users should reach any major content area within three clicks from anywhere on the site. However, don't sacrifice information architecture for arbitrary click counts—clarity trumps brevity.
   144→
   145→**Breadcrumb Psychology**
   146→Breadcrumbs reduce cognitive load by showing location in information hierarchy. Always include them on inner pages (articles, categories, products).
   147→
   148→**Expected Placement**
   149→Place navigation elements where users expect them:
   150→- Logo: Top-left (LTR) / Top-right (RTL)
   151→- Primary nav: Horizontal bar below header
   152→- Search: Header area, visible icon
   153→- Social links: Header or footer
   154→- Newsletter: Footer or dedicated section
   155→
   156→### Interaction Feedback
   157→
   158→**Immediate Response**
   159→All interactive elements must provide feedback within 100ms:
   160→- Hover states for mouse users
   161→- Focus states for keyboard users
   162→- Active/pressed states for touch users
   163→- Loading indicators for async operations
   164→
   165→**Progressive Disclosure**
   166→Show essential information first; reveal details on demand:
   167→- Expandable FAQ sections
   168→- "Read more" links for long excerpts
   169→- Collapsible technical details
   170→- Tooltip previews for linked content
   171→
   172→**Error Prevention Over Error Recovery**
   173→Design forms and interactions that prevent errors:
   174→- Input validation before submission
   175→- Clear label and placeholder text
   176→- Confirmation for destructive actions
   177→- Undo capability for reversible actions
   178→
   179→### Content Discovery
   180→
   181→**Related Content Placement**
   182→Position related articles at natural stopping points:
   183→- Inline recommendations mid-article (after 30% scroll)
   184→- End-of-article recommendations (primary placement)
   185→- Sidebar recommendations (secondary placement)
   186→
   187→**Category Exploration**
   188→Help users discover content by topic:
   189→- Clear category labels with descriptive names
   190→- Category pages with featured content
   191→- Cross-linking between related categories
   192→- Breadcrumbs showing hierarchical relationships
   193→
   194→**Search Behavior Support**
   195→Accommodate different search behaviors:
   196→- Instant results as user types
   197→- Search suggestions based on popular queries
   198→- Faceted filtering for content types
   199→- "Did you mean?" corrections for typos

   203→## 3. UI Principles
   204→
   205→### Visual Hierarchy
   206→
   207→**The F-Pattern for Text-Heavy Pages**
   208→Users scan in an F-pattern on content pages:
   209→- Horizontal movement across top (headline, primary nav)
   210→- Second horizontal movement (subheadings, secondary nav)
   211→- Vertical movement down left side (first words of paragraphs)
   212→- Place important elements along these scanning paths
   213→
   214→**The Z-Pattern for Landing Pages**
   215→For promotional or landing pages:
   216→- Eye moves top-left to top-right
   217→- Diagonally down to bottom-left
   218→- Across to bottom-right
   219→- Place CTAs at pattern endpoints
   220→
   221→**Size Indicates Importance**
   222→Larger elements attract more attention. Use scale deliberately:
   223→- Headlines: Largest (H1: 2.5-3rem)
   224→- Subheadings: Large (H2: 1.8-2.2rem)
   225→- Body: Medium (16-18px)
   226→- Meta/supporting: Small (14px or less)
   227→
   228→**Contrast Creates Separation**
   229→Use contrast differences to create layers:
   230→- Highest contrast: Primary CTA, alerts
   231→- High contrast: Headlines, important info
   232→- Medium contrast: Body text
   233→- Low contrast: Secondary info, metadata
   234→
   235→### Layout Principles
   236→
   237→**Grid Systems**
   238→Use a 12-column grid for flexibility:
   239→- Homepage: Complex multi-column layouts
   240→- Article: Single column + optional sidebar
   241→- Archive: 2-3 column grid for cards
   242→- Mobile: Always single column
   243→
   244→**Alignment & Proximity**
   245→- Align related items to create visual groups
   246→- Group related items closer than unrelated items
   247→- Left-align body text for readability
   248→- Center only for short headlines and CTAs
   249→
   250→**Asymmetric Balance**
   251→Symmetric layouts feel static; asymmetric feels dynamic:
   252→- Use a dominant element (hero image, large headline)
   253→- Balance with smaller supporting elements
   254→- Maintain visual weight distribution even when asymmetrical
   255→
   256→**Negative Space as Design Element**
   257→White space is not empty—it's an active design element:
   258→- Breathing room around content blocks
   259→- Margins that separate distinct sections
   260→- Padding that prevents cramped feeling
   261→- Generous whitespace signals quality and focus
   262→
   263→### Component Consistency
   264→
   265→**Atomic Design Methodology**
   266→Structure components in five levels:
   267→1. **Atoms**: Buttons, inputs, labels, icons
   268→2. **Molecules**: Search bars, card headers, form fields
   269→3. **Organisms**: Headers, cards, navigation bars
   270→4. **Templates**: Page layouts with placeholder content
   271→5. **Pages**: Templates with real content
   272→
   273→**Consistent Component API**
   274→Every component should have predictable props:
   275→- `variant`: Visual style variations
   276→- `size`: Size options (sm, md, lg)
   277→- `disabled`: Disabled state
   278→- `loading`: Loading state
   279→- `className`: Escape hatch for custom styling
   280→
   281→**State Management**
   282→Define all component states:
   283→- Default/Normal
   284→- Hover/Focus
   285→- Active/Selected
   286→- Disabled
   287→- Loading/Error
   288→- Empty (no data)

   292→## 4. Typography System
   293→
   294→### Font Selection Strategy
   295→
   296→**Primary Font (Body Text)**
   297→Choose fonts optimized for screen reading:
   298→- Characteristics: Tall x-height, open counters, clear letterforms
   299→- Recommended categories: Humanist sans-serif, Grotesque sans-serif
   300→- Examples: Inter, Source Sans Pro, Lato, Nunito Sans, Plus Jakarta Sans
   301→- Avoid: Display fonts, condensed faces, decorative fonts for body
   302→
   303→**Secondary Font (Headings)**
   304→Can be more expressive but must remain readable:
   305→- Characteristics: Distinctive personality, good weight range
   306→- Recommended categories: Geometric sans-serif, Modern serif, Slab serif
   307→- Examples: DM Serif Display, Playfair Display, Fraunces, Space Grotesk, Sora
   308→- Must pair harmoniously with primary font
   309→
   310→**Tertiary Font (Code/Accents)**
   311→Monospace for code, numbers, or special callouts:
   312→- Examples: JetBrains Mono, Fira Code, Source Code Pro
   313→- Use sparingly for technical content or data display
   314→
   315→**Font Pairing Rules**
   316→1. Limit to 2-3 font families maximum
   317→2. Pair contrasting classifications (sans + serif works well)
   318→3. Match x-heights approximately
   319→4. Ensure similar color temperature (both warm or both neutral)
   320→5. Test at actual sizes before finalizing
   321→
   322→### Type Scale
   323→
   324→**Modular Scale System**
   325→Use a ratio-based scale for consistency:
   326→
   327→| Element       | Size (rem) | Line Height | Weight | Letter Spacing |
   328→|---------------|------------|-------------|--------|----------------|
   329→| Hero (H1)     | 3.0 - 3.5  | 1.1 - 1.2   | 700-800 | -0.02em - -0.03em |
   330→| Page Title    | 2.25 - 2.5 | 1.2 - 1.25  | 700    | -0.01em - -0.02em |
   331→| Section (H2)  | 1.875 - 2  | 1.25 - 1.3  | 600-700 | -0.01em |
   332→| Subsection    | 1.5 - 1.75 | 1.3 - 1.35  | 600    | 0              |
   333→| Card Title    | 1.25 - 1.5 | 1.35 - 1.4  | 600    | 0              |
   334→| Body Large    | 1.125 - 1.25| 1.6 - 1.7   | 400    | 0              |
   335→| Body          | 1 (16px)   | 1.65 - 1.75 | 400    | 0              |
   336→| Body Small    | 0.875      | 1.6         | 400    | 0              |
   337→| Caption       | 0.75       | 1.5         | 400    | 0.01em - 0.02em |
   338→| Overline      | 0.6875     | 1.4         | 500-600 | 0.05em - 0.1em  |
   339→
   340→**Responsive Typography**
   341→Adjust sizes for viewport:
   342→
   343→```css
   344→/* Fluid typography formula */
   345→body {
   346→  font-size: clamp(1rem, 0.95rem + 0.25vw, 1.125rem);
   347→}
   348→
   349→h1 {
   350→  font-size: clamp(2rem, 1.5rem + 2.5vw, 3.5rem);
   351→}
   352→```
   353→
   354→### Typography Best Practices
   355→
   356→**Line Height Guidelines**
   357→- Headings: 1.1 - 1.3 (tighter for impact)
   358→- Body text: 1.65 - 1.8 (comfortable reading)
   359→- Long-form content: 1.7 - 1.85 (reduced eye strain)
   360→- Never below 1.3 for body, never above 2.2
   361→
   362→**Paragraph Width Control**
   363→```css
   364→.article-content {
   365→  max-width: 70ch; /* Optimal reading measure */
   366→  margin-left: auto;
   367→  margin-right: auto;
   368→}
   369→```
   370→
   371→**Text Rendering Optimization**
   372→```css
   373→body {
   374→  -webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased;
   375→  -moz-osx-font-smoothing: grayscale;
   376→  text-rendering: optimizeLegibility;
   377→  font-feature-settings: 'kern' 1, 'liga' 1;
   378→}
   379→```
   380→
   381→**Orphan & Widow Control**
   382→- Avoid single words on last line (orphans)
   383→- Avoid single lines at top of new page/column (widows)
   384→- CSS solution limited; prefer editorial awareness
   385→
   386→**Emphasis Hierarchy**
   387→1. Bold: Key terms, first use of important concepts
   388→2. Italics: Publication titles, foreign terms, subtle emphasis
   389→3. ALL CAPS: Very sparingly, for labels/categories only
   390→4. Color: For interactive elements or status indicators
   391→5. Underline: Links only (never for emphasis)

   395→## 5. Color System
   396→
   397→### Color Philosophy
   398→
   399→**Color Roles, Not Just Colors**
   400→Assign semantic roles to colors:
   401→- **Primary**: Brand identity, primary actions, active states
   402→- **Secondary**: Supporting brand elements, secondary actions
   403→- **Accent**: Highlights, CTAs, important notifications
   404→- **Neutral**: Backgrounds, text, borders, structure
   405→- **Semantic**: Success, warning, error, info (universal meanings)
   406→
   407→**Accessibility-First Palette**
   408→Build palettes that meet WCAG AA from the start:
   409→- All text/background combinations: minimum 4.5:1 ratio
   410→- Large text (18px+): minimum 3:1 ratio
   411→- Interactive elements: 3:1 against adjacent colors
   412→- Focus indicators: 3:1 against adjacent colors
   413→
   414→### Neutral Scale
   415→
   416→Build from a base neutral, extending in both directions:
   417→
   418→```
   419→Gray Scale (Example):
   420→├── gray-50:   #FAFAFA  (Subtle backgrounds)
   421→├── gray-100:  #F5F5F5  (Page backgrounds, card backgrounds)
   422→├── gray-200:  #EEEEEE  (Borders, dividers)
   423→├── gray-300:  #E0E0E0  (Disabled states, light borders)
   424→├── gray-400:  #BDBDBD  (Placeholder text, icons)
   425→├── gray-500:  #9E9E9E  (Secondary text, muted elements)
   426→├── gray-600:  #757575  (Supporting text, captions)
   427→├── gray-700:  #616161  (Body text on light surfaces)
   428→├── gray-800:  #424242  (Headings, emphasized text)
   429→└── gray-900:  #212121  (Primary text, strongest emphasis)
   430→```
   431→
   432→### Primary Color Generation
   433→
   434→From a single hue, generate a complete palette:
   435→
   436→```
   437→Primary Scale (Example using emerald):
   438→├── primary-50:   Lightest tint (light mode backgrounds)
   439→├── primary-100:  Very light tint (hover states)
   440→├── primary-200:  Light tint (subtle highlights)
   441→├── primary-300:  Medium-light (secondary elements)
   442→├── primary-400:  Medium (supporting accents)
   443→├── primary-500:  Base color (brand primary)
   444→├── primary-600:  Dark shade (default dark text, hover states)
   445→├── primary-700:  Darker (active states, headings)
   446→├── primary-800:  Dark (emphasis, dark mode accents)
   447→└── primary-900:  Darkest (strongest emphasis)
   448→```
   449→
   450→### Semantic Colors
   451→
   452→Universal meanings that transcend brand:
   453→
   454→| Role      | Usage                          | Example Hue  | Contrast Req. |
   455→|-----------|--------------------------------|--------------|---------------|
   456→| Success   | Confirmations, positive states | Green        | 3:1 minimum   |
   457→| Warning   | Cautions, attention needed     | Amber/Yellow | 3:1 minimum   |
   458→| Error     | Errors, destructive actions    | Red          | 4.5:1 minimum |
   459→| Info      | Informational, help            | Blue         | 3:1 minimum   |
   460→
   461→**Important**: Semantic colors must be distinguishable by color blindness. Never rely solely on hue—always pair with icons or text labels.
   462→
   463→### Dark Mode Considerations
   464→
   465→**Not Just Invert**
   466→Dark mode requires intentional design:
   467→- Pure black (#000000) is too harsh; use dark grays (#1a1a1a to #171717)
   468→- Reduce overall luminance, not just swap light/dark
   469→- Desaturate slightly for reduced eye strain
   470→- Increase contrast slightly compared to light mode
   471→- Add subtle elevation through lighter surfaces
   472→
   473→**Surface Elevations (Dark Mode)**
   474→```
   475→├── Surface 0 (Base):    #121212 or #0a0a0a
   476→├── Surface 1 (Raised):  #1E1E1E or #141414
   477→├── Surface 2 (Overlay): #2D2D2D or #1c1c1c
   478→└── Surface 3 (Modal):   #3C3C3C or #242424
   479→```

   500→## 6. Spacing System
   501→
   502→### Base Unit System
   503→
   504→Establish a base unit (typically 4px or 8px) and build all spacing from multiples:
   505→
   506→```
   507→Base Unit: 4px (0.25rem)
   508→
   509→Spacing Scale:
   510→├── 0:     0px      (reset, flush)
   511→├── 0.5:   2px      (tight, icon gaps)
   512→├── 1:     4px      (inline element spacing)
   513→├── 1.5:   6px      (rare, transitional)
   514→├── 2:     8px      (small gaps, button padding-y)
   515→├── 2.5:   10px     (transitional)
   516→├── 3:     12px     (standard gap, form padding)
   517→├── 4:     16px     (card padding, section padding-sm)
   518→├── 5:     20px     (component gaps)
   519→├── 6:     24px     (section padding-md)
   520→├── 8:     32px     (section padding-lg)
   521→├── 10:    40px     (section padding-xl)
   522→├── 12:    48px     (large section gaps)
   523→├── 16:    64px     (page section gaps)
   524→├── 20:    80px     (major section divisions)
   525→├── 24:    96px     (page-level spacing)
   526→└── 32:    128px    (hero-level spacing)
   527→```
   528→
   529→### Contextual Spacing Rules
   530→
   531→**Element Internal Padding**
   532→- Buttons: 8-16px horizontal, 6-12px vertical
   533→- Cards: 16-24px (mobile), 24-32px (desktop)
   534→- Form inputs: 10-14px vertical, 12-16px horizontal
   535→- Badges/tags: 4-8px vertical, 8-12px horizontal
   536→
   537→**Element External Margins**
   538→- Between paragraphs: 16-24px
   539→- Between sections: 48-96px
   540→- Between cards in grid: 16-24px
   541→- Around main content: 24-48px (mobile), 48-80px (desktop)
   542→
   543→**Layout Spacing**
   544→- Container max-width gutters: 16-24px (mobile), 24-40px (desktop)
   545→- Grid gaps: 16-24px standard, 8-12px tight
   546→- Sidebar from main: 32-48px
   547→- Sticky element from viewport edge: 16-24px

   550→

   553→```css

   556→  padding: 64px 24px; /* Desktop */
   557→}
   558→
   559→@media (max-width: 768px) {
   560→  .section {
   561→    padding: 48px 16px; /* Tablet */
   562→  }
   563→}
   564→
   565→@media (max-width: 480px) {
   566→  .section {
   567→    padding: 32px 12px; /* Mobile */
   568→  }
   569→}

   790→## 8. Accessibility (WCAG AA)
   791→
   792→### Non-Negotiable Requirements
   793→
   794→**Perceivable**
   795→
   796→*Text Alternatives*
   797→- All images have meaningful alt text
   798→- Decorative images use alt="" (empty)
   799→- Icons in buttons have aria-label or visible text
   800→- Charts/graphs have text descriptions
   801→- Video has captions/transcript available
   802→
   803→*Color & Contrast*
   804→- Normal text: minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio
   805→- Large text (18px+ or 14px+ bold): minimum 3:1
   806→- Interactive elements: 3:1 against adjacent color
   807→- Focus indicators: 3:1 against adjacent
   808→- Don't convey information with color alone
   809→
   810→*Resizable Text*
   811→- Text scales to 200% without loss of function
   812→- Use relative units (rem, em, %)
   813→- Viewport meta tag present
   814→- No horizontal scrolling at 320px zoomed
   815→
   816→**Operable**
   817→
   818→*Keyboard Navigation*
   819→- All interactive elements reachable via Tab
   820→- Visible focus indicators on all focused elements
   821→- Logical tab order (DOM order = visual order)
   822→- Skip navigation link (visible on focus)
   823→- Modal focus trapping
   824→- Escape closes modals/menus
   825→- Arrow keys for custom widgets (carousel, tabs)
   826→
   827→*Time-Based Content*
   828→- No time limits for reading
   829→- If required, provide extension mechanism
   830→- Auto-playing content has pause control
   831→- Moving/blinking can be stopped
   832→
   833→*Seizure Safety*
   834→- Nothing flashes more than 3 times per second
   835→- No rapid color transitions
   836→- Respect prefers-reduced-motion
   837→
   838→**Understandable**
   839→
   840→*Readable Content*
   841→- Page language declared (lang attribute)
   842→- Language changes marked
   843→- Unusual words explained
   844→- Abbreviations expanded on first use
   845→- Reading level appropriate for audience
   846→
   847→*Predictable*
   848→- Navigation consistent across pages
   849→- Same components behave same way
   850→- Context changes only on user initiation
   851→- Clear labeling of all controls
   852→
   853→*Input Assistance*
   854→- Labels associated with all inputs
   855→- Instructions provided before input needed
   856→- Error messages identify problems clearly
   857→- Error prevention for legal/financial commitments
   858→- Suggestions for correcting errors
   859→
   860→**Robust**
   861→
   862→*Compatible*
   863→- Valid HTML (use semantic elements)
   864→- ARIA used correctly (not as replacement for semantics)
   865→- Custom components follow WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices
   866→- Tested with actual assistive technology
   867→
   868→### Accessibility Testing Checklist
   869→
   870→- [ ] Screen reader test (VoiceOver, NVDA, JAWS)
   871→- [ ] Keyboard-only navigation test
   872→- [ ] Color contrast audit (all combinations)
   873→- [ ] Focus management review
   874→- [ ] Zoom to 200% functionality check
   875→- [ ] Mobile VoiceOver/TalkBack test
   876→- [ ] Automated testing (axe, WAVE, Lighthouse)

   898→    <!-- Primary content -->
   899→

   901→      <header>

   903→        <!-- Article meta -->
   904→      </header>
   905→

   917→    <!-- Site footer -->
   918→  </footer>

   922→
   923→### Heading Hierarchy Rules
   924→
   925→**Single H1 Per Page**
   926→- Homepage: Site name or tagline
   927→- Article: Article title
   928→- Category: Category name
   929→- Other: Most prominent heading
   930→
   931→**Proper Nesting**
   932→```
   933→H1 (one per page)
   934→├── H2 (sections)
   935→│   ├── H3 (subsections)
   936→│   │   └── H4 (detail sections - rarely needed)
   937→│   └── H3
   938→└── H2
   939→```
   940→
   941→**Never skip levels** (no H1 → H3)
   942→
   943→### Structured Data (Schema.org)
   944→
   945→**Article Markup (Required)**
   946→```json
   947→{
   948→  "@context": "https://schema.org",
   949→  "@type": "Article",
   950→  "headline": "Article Title",
   951→  "image": ["https://example.com/image.jpg"],
   952→  "datePublished": "2024-01-15T08:00:00+00:00",
   953→  "dateModified": "2024-01-20T10:30:00+00:00",
   954→  "author": {
   955→    "@type": "Person",
   956→    "name": "Author Name",
   957→    "url": "/author/author-slug"
   958→  },
   959→  "publisher": {
   960→    "@type": "Organization",
   961→    "name": "Site Name",
   962→    "logo": {
   963→      "@type": "ImageObject",
   964→      "url": "https://example.com/logo.png"
   965→    }
   966→  }
   967→}
   968→```
   969→
   970→**BlogPosting vs Article**
   971→- Use `BlogPosting` for informal, blog-style content
   972→- Use `Article` for news, formal journalism
   973→- Use `HowTo` for tutorials with steps
   974→- Use `FAQPage` for Q&A content
   975→- Use `Recipe` for recipes (rich results opportunity)
   976→
   977→**BreadcrumbList Markup**
   978→```json
   979→{
   980→  "@context": "https://schema.org",
   981→  "@type": "BreadcrumbList",
   982→  "itemListElement": [
   983→    { "@type": "ListItem", "position": 1, "name": "Home", "item": "/" },
   984→    { "@type": "ListItem", "position": 2, "name": "Category", "item": "/category" },
   985→    { "@type": "ListItem", "position": 3, "name": "Article" }
   986→  ]
   987→}
   988→```
   989→
   990→### URL Structure Best Practices
   991→
   992→**Pattern**: `/{content-type}/{category-slug}/{post-slug}/`
   993→
   994→Examples:
   995→- `/blog/gardening/how-to-propagate-monstera/`
   996→- `/guides/indoor-plants/best-low-light-plants/`
   997→- `/recipes/desserts/chocolate-lava-cake/`
   998→
   999→**URL Rules**
  1000→- Lowercase only
  1001→- Hyphens separate words (no underscores)
  1002→- No query parameters for content URLs
  1003→- Keep under 60 characters when possible
  1004→- Include primary keyword naturally
  1005→- Remove stop words (a, an, the) unless needed for clarity
  1006→
  1007→### Internal Linking Architecture
  1008→
  1009→**Contextual Links**
  1010→- 3-5 internal links per 1000 words (natural, not forced)
  1011→- Link to relevant, high-value pages
  1012→- Use descriptive anchor text (not "click here")
  1013→- Vary anchor text for same destination
  1014→- Open external links in new tab (with indication)
  1015→
  1016→**Hub Page Strategy**
  1017→- Create comprehensive pillar/hub content
  1018→- Link cluster topics to hub page
  1019→- Hub links out to all related articles
  1020→- Articles link back to hub
  1021→- Creates topical authority signals
  1022→
  1023→**Navigation Links**
  1024→- Categories in main nav (limit to 6-8)
  1025→- Recent/popular in sidebar or footer
  1026→- Related posts at article end
  1027→- Breadcrumbs on all inner pages
  1028→- HTML sitemap linked from footer

  1232→- 300×1050 skyscraper (for tall sidebars)
  1233→- Sticky sidebar ad (follows scroll, one per view)
  1234→- Maximum 3-4 ads in sidebar total
  1235→
  1236→**Between-Content Ads**
  1237→- Between article end and comments
  1238→- Between related post rows (every 3-4 cards)
  1239→- In infinite scroll (between content loads)
  1240→
  1241→### Ad Layout Technical Requirements
  1242→
  1243→**Ad Container Specifications**
  1244→```css
  1245→.ad-container {
  1246→  min-height: 90px;  /* Prevent CLS from ad loading */
  1247→  min-width: 728px;  /* Or responsive */
  1248→  display: flex;
  1249→  align-items: center;
  1250→  justify-content: center;
  1251→  background: #f5f5f5; /* Placeholder */
  1252→}
  1253→
  1254→/* Responsive ad wrapper */
  1255→.ad-responsive {
  1256→  max-width: 100%;
  1257→  overflow: hidden;
  1258→}
  1259→```
  1260→
  1261→**Non-Intrusive Integration**
  1262→- Ads match content background where possible
  1263→- Clear but subtle "Advertisement" label
  1264→- Adequate spacing from content (16px+)
  1265→- No animation/movement (distracting, against policy)
  1266→- No ads that look like content (deceptive)
  1267→
  1268→**Mobile Ad Rules**
  1269→- Fewer ads than desktop (quality over quantity)
  1270→- Anchor ads (sticky bottom) - one only
  1271→- In-article native ads preferred
  1272→- No interstitials covering content
  1273→- Adequate tap targets between ad and content
  1274→
  1275→### AdSense Policy Compliance
  1276→
  1277→**Content Rules**
  1278→- Adequate content between ads (don't "ad farm")
  1279→- No ads on empty/thin pages
  1280→- No ads behind login/paywall
  1281→- No misleading ad placement near navigation
  1282→
  1283→**Prohibited Placements**
  1284→- Ads that could be accidentally clicked
  1285→- Ads in sliders/carousels (usually)
  1286→- Pop-ups/pop-unders (against policy)
  1287→- Ads that obscure content

  1291→## 14. Mobile-First Rules
  1292→
  1293→### Development Approach
  1294→
  1295→**Write Mobile Styles First**
  1296→```css
  1297→/* Mobile-first: base styles are mobile */
  1298→.card {
  1299→  display: flex;
  1300→  flex-direction: column;
  1301→  padding: 16px;
  1302→}
  1303→
  1304→/* Enhance for larger screens */
  1305→@media (min-width: 768px) {
  1306→  .card {
  1307→    flex-direction: row;
  1308→    padding: 24px;
  1309→  }
  1310→}
  1311→```
  1312→
  1313→**Mobile Constraints as Design Foundation**
  1314→- Single column layouts
  1315→- Touch-optimized targets (44px minimum)
  1316→- Simplified navigation (hamburger menu)
  1317→- Reduced visual complexity
  1318→- Prioritized content (less visible at once)
  1319→
  1320→### Touch Interface Design
  1321→
  1322→**Touch Target Sizes**
  1323→- Primary actions: 48×48px minimum
  1324→- Secondary actions: 44×44px minimum
  1325→- Inline actions: 32×32px (with surrounding padding)
  1326→- Dense lists: 40px height minimum row
  1327→
  1328→**Touch Spacing**
  1329→- 8px minimum between touch targets
  1330→- 12px recommended for frequently used
  1331→- 16px for critical actions (prevent mis-taps)
  1332→
  1333→**Gesture Support**
  1334→- Swipe for carousels/galleries
  1335→- Pull-to-refresh (where appropriate)
  1336→- Long-press for contextual menus
  1337→- Pinch-to-zoom for images/maps
  1338→
  1339→### Mobile Content Prioritization
  1340→
  1341→**Progressive Content Disclosure**
  1342→1. Critical (always visible): Title, hero, primary CTA
  1343→2. Important (quick scroll): Key info, navigation
  1344→3. Useful (further scroll): Details, related content
  1345→4. Secondary (available): Footer, extras, archives
  1346→
  1347→**Mobile-Specific Simplifications**
  1348→- Condensed navigation (fewer items visible)
  1349→- Collapsible sections (FAQ, details)
  1350→- Bottom-anchored actions (sticky CTA)
  1351→- Simplified forms (fewer fields, smarter defaults)
  1352→- Tabbed interfaces (save vertical space)
  1353→
  1354→### Mobile Performance Budget
  1355→
  1356→**Target Metrics**
  1357→- First Contentful Paint: < 1.5s
  1358→- Time to Interactive: < 3.5s
  1359→- Cumulative Layout Shift: < 0.1
  1360→- Total page weight: < 1.5MB initial load
  1361→
  1362→**Performance Techniques**
  1363→- Lazy load images below fold
  1364→- Critical CSS inlined
  1365→- Deferred/non-critical JavaScript
  1366→- Responsive images (srcset)
  1367→- Font loading optimization (font-display: swap)
  1368→- Service worker caching strategy

  1372→## 15. Responsive Rules
  1373→
  1374→### Breakpoint System
  1375→
  1376→**Standard Breakpoints**
  1377→
  1378→| Name     | Width      | Target Devices                  |
  1379→|----------|------------|---------------------------------|
  1380→| `xs`     | 0-479px    | Small phones                    |
  1381→| `sm`     | 480-639px  | Large phones                    |
  1382→| `md`     | 640-767px  | Tablets portrait                |
  1383→| `lg`     | 768-1023px | Tablets landscape, small laptops|
  1384→| `xl`     | 1024-1279px| Desktops                        |
  1385→| `2xl`    | 1280px+    | Large desktops                   |
  1386→
  1387→**Naming Convention**
  1388→Use logical names, not device names:
  1389→- ✅ `min-width: 768px`
  1390→- ❌ `@tablet`
  1391→- ✅ `@media (min-width: 64em)`
  1392→- ❌ `@media ipad`
  1393→
  1394→### Responsive Patterns
  1395→
  1396→**Container Pattern**
  1397→```css
  1398→.container {
  1399→  width: 100%;
  1400→  max-width: 1280px;
  1401→  margin: 0 auto;
  1402→  padding: 0 1rem; /* Mobile */
  1403→}
  1404→
  1405→@media (min-width: 768px) {
  1406→  .container {
  1407→    padding: 0 2rem; /* Tablet+ */
  1408→  }
  1409→}
  1410→```
  1411→
  1412→**Grid Responsiveness**
  1413→```
  1414→Mobile (default):  1 column
  1415→Tablet (md):       2 columns  
  1416→Desktop (lg):      3 columns
  1417→Large (xl):        4 columns (sidebar + 3 content)
  1418→```
  1419→
  1420→**Typography Scaling**
  1421→- Mobile: Slightly larger relative sizes (easier to read)
  1422→- Tablet: Standard scale
  1423→- Desktop: Can be slightly smaller (viewing distance)
  1424→
  1425→**Navigation Transformation**
  1426→- Mobile: Hamburger → full-screen overlay or slide-out
  1427→- Tablet: Hamburger or condensed horizontal
  1428→- Desktop: Full horizontal with dropdowns
  1429→
  1430→**Sidebar Behavior**
  1431→- Mobile: Below content or hidden (tab access)
  1432→- Tablet: Below content or narrow sidebar
  1433→- Desktop: Right or left sidebar (fixed or sticky)
  1434→
  1435→### Responsive Images

  1474→- Shrinks on scroll (height reduction)

  1477→- Reserve space to prevent layout shift
  1478→- Allow disabling for accessibility preference
  1479→
  1480→**Transparent Header**
  1481→- Overlays hero image initially
  1482→- Becomes solid on scroll
  1483→- Ensure text contrast over hero
  1484→- Not recommended for content-heavy sites
  1485→
  1486→**Always-Visible Elements**
  1487→Even in compact sticky state:
  1488→- Logo (may shrink)
  1489→- Navigation (may become icons only)
  1490→- Search access
  1491→- At least one CTA
  1492→

  1494→
  1495→**Maximum Items**
  1496→- Desktop: 6-8 top-level items
  1497→- More items → "More" dropdown or reorganize
  1498→- Mobile: All in hamburger menu
  1499→
  1500→**Dropdown Menus**
  1501→- Appear on hover (desktop) and click (mobile)
  1502→- Mega-menu for many subcategories
  1503→- Clear visual separation from header
  1504→- Keyboard accessible (arrow keys)
  1505→- Close on outside click or Escape
  1506→
  1507→**Current Page Indicator**
  1508→- Underline, background change, or bold
