    23→    23→

    25→    25→
    26→    26→This palette is built on **accessibility-first** principles (WCAG AA compliance), with **semantic naming** for maintainability, and **CSS custom properties** for easy theming.

   271→   271→
   272→   272→| Token | Usage | Example |
   273→   273→|-------|-------|---------|
   274→   274→| `--bg-primary` | Page background | `<body>` |
   275→   275→| `--bg-secondary` | Cards, sidebars | `<article>`, `<aside>` |
   276→   276→| `--bg-tertiary` | Code blocks, hover rows | `<pre>`, table hover |
   277→   277→| `--text-primary` | Body text, headings | `<p>`, `<h1-h6>` |
   278→   278→| `--text-secondary` | Metadata, captions | Date, author name |
   279→   279→| `--text-tertiary` | Disabled, placeholder | `::placeholder` |
   280→   280→| `--text-link` | Links | `<a>` |
   281→   281→| `--border-default` | Input borders, dividers | `<input>`, `<hr>` |
   282→   282→| `--border-focus` | Focus rings | `:focus-visible` |
   283→   283→| `--interactive-primary` | Primary buttons | `.btn-primary` |
   284→   284→| `--interactive-danger` | Delete, error actions | `.btn-danger` |
